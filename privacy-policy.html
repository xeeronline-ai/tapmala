TapMala – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

TapMala ("we", "our" or "us") is developed by XEER Technology. This Privacy Policy explains how the TapMala app (package com.xeer.tapmala) handles information when you use it.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account to use TapMala.
  • Your jap counts, history, profile and settings are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads (see section 5).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide app features, TapMala stores the following locally on your phone:

  • Profile details you choose to enter, such as your display name, optional mobile number and profile photo
  • Jap and mala counts, session history, streaks and statistics
  • App settings such as language, theme, sound, vibration, mala target, auto mode speed and reminders
  • Selected jap audio, and any audio you record or images and audio files you add
  • Backup or export files you create from Settings

We cannot see this information. It stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

3. Permissions we request

  • Notifications: for daily jap reminders, streak alerts, mala completion, weekly summary and tithi or festival reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Microphone: only when you choose to record your own jap chant. The recording is saved on your device.
  • Photos / media: only when you choose a profile picture or a cover image for custom audio.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback while counting.
  • Background audio, wake lock and battery optimisation exemption: to play jap audio in auto mode and to deliver reminders reliably.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: to load ads, load fonts, open links and share content.

You can deny or revoke any permission in your device settings. Some features may not work without the related permission.

4. How we use information

Information stored in TapMala is used only to count and track your jap sessions, show history and statistics, play audio, save your preferences, send reminders you turn on and create backups when you ask for them.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

TapMala shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), TapMala asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google or Ads settings.

6. Other third-party services

  • Google Fonts: fonts may be downloaded over the internet.
  • Google Play and system share options: when you rate, share or contact us, the app or service you pick handles that data under its own policy.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Information leaves your device only when you choose to share it (for example sharing a mala completion message, a backup file, or emailing us) or as described in the Advertising section.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove custom audio and images in the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall TapMala to delete all locally stored data. Because we do not keep your app data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

TapMala is a general-audience devotional app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored within the app, but no device or storage method is completely secure. We recommend using the backup feature to keep a copy of important data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
